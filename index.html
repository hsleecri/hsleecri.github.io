About

Hi! I'm a second-year Ph.D. student in the Wm Michael Barnes ’64 Department of Industrial & Systems Engineering at Texas A&M University, where I'm fortunate to be part of the Jeong Research Group.

With a background in both mechanical and industrial engineering, I am driven to tackle practical challenges in manufacturing systems using machine learning (ML). Specifically, my research focuses on integrating domain knowledge into ML models and mitigating their training instability in real-world applications. Ultimately, I hope to pursue high-impact research that makes a meaningful contribution to the real world.

My research interests include:

  • Advanced manufacturing
  • Process monitoring and control

Methodologies

  • Machine learning
  • Deep learning
  • Physics-informed neural networks

Research

Physics-informed machine learning for manufacturing systems

  • PINN surrogates for temperature fields in metal additive manufacturing (AM)
  • Generalization across process parameters, materials, and laser paths
  • Inverse modeling and more stable, effective, and efficient PINN optimization

Related: Material-agnostic PINN · Adaptive sampling · Multi-domain PINNs

Data-driven process monitoring and control

  • Robust unsupervised anomaly detection under contaminated in-situ monitoring data
  • Real-time defect classification on edge devices
  • Multimodal sensor fusion to predict composition and hardness
  • Time-series classification and process-degradation modeling for adaptive control

Related: Robust anomaly detection · Edge defect classification · Composition and hardness · Nonstationary HMM · Time-series imaging
